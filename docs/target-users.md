# Target Users

## Primary Users

### Students
Students are frequent targets of phishing through school email, financial aid scams, fake account warnings, and impersonation attempts. PhishTix is designed to support learning and safer decision-making.

### Everyday Users
Many non-technical users struggle to evaluate suspicious texts, emails, and app-based messages. PhishTix aims to simplify that review process and help users identify potential threats before interacting with them.

### Entry-Level SOC Learners
PhishTix also reflects analyst-style phishing review concepts, making it useful as a portfolio project for those learning cybersecurity and incident triage.

## Organizational Use

### Businesses and Enterprises
Organizations of all sizes face constant phishing attempts targeting employees, credentials, and financial operations. PhishTix can support awareness and structured review of suspicious communications within workplace environments.

Potential use cases include:
- employee phishing awareness support
- suspicious message analysis before escalation
- internal security training demonstrations
- awareness programs for non-technical staff
- educational examples for security teams

## Secondary Use Cases

PhishTix may also support:

- classroom demonstrations
- cybersecurity seminars and presentations
- faculty showcases
- portfolio review by recruiters
- discussions around phishing defense and user-focused cybersecurity tools
